# Loan Lifecycle

## What is a Loan?

A loan is money borrowed from a bank or financial institution that must be repaid over time with interest.

## Why Banks Evaluate Loans

Banks evaluate loan applications to reduce the risk of losing money if a customer fails to repay the loan.

## What is Loan Default?

Loan default occurs when a customer fails to repay the loan according to the agreed terms.

## Role of FRIS

FRIS helps loan officers by analyzing customer financial information, predicting loan default risk, explaining the prediction, and providing recommendations.

The final decision is always made by the loan officer.
